# NCURA Annual 2026 Concurrent Session Abstract

**Title:** Foundational AI Literacy for Research Administrators

## Description

This session introduces research administration professionals to foundational AI concepts, terminology, and practical applications in administrative workflows. Participants will build their AI literacy by gaining a clear understanding of how AI works, its capabilities, and its limitations. Through interactive exercises and discussions, attendees will learn to select appropriate AI models, break complex tasks into manageable components, and identify areas where AI and automation can add value. They will also learn techniques for designing high-quality prompts, evaluating AI outputs for accuracy and reliability, and applying advanced strategies such as iterative refinement. Building on these practical skills, the session will then explore ethical, transparency, and governance considerations, emphasizing that these skills are broadly applicable across different AI tools and platforms.

## Learning Objectives

1. Explain foundational AI concepts and terminology relevant to research administration.
2. Identify tasks and workflows in research administration where AI and automation can add value.
3. Select appropriate AI models and break complex tasks into manageable components across different AI tools and platforms.
4. Design effective and accurate AI prompts for specific administrative tasks.
5. Evaluate AI outputs for accuracy, reliability, and appropriateness, and apply iterative refinement strategies broadly across AI systems.
6. Be prepared to discuss and address ethical, transparency, and governance considerations in AI-assisted workflows.

## Prerequisites

None
