# AI Literacy for Research Administrators

Course-level overview. If you have any questions, please contact AI4RA Program Manager, Dashiell Tyler at dashiellt@uidaho.edu.

## Course Description

This asynchronous course introduces research administrators to artificial intelligence (AI) concepts, tools, and applications relevant to research administration. Participants will learn key terminology, explore ethical and compliance implications, and develop practical skills in prompt development for responsible and effective AI use. Through real-world examples, interactive games, and video content, participants will build confidence in using AI to enhance productivity while maintaining compliance and integrity.

## Learning Objectives

1. Define foundational AI concepts and terminology relevant to research administration.
2. Evaluate ethical, legal, and regulatory considerations associated with AI use.
3. Apply effective prompt engineering techniques to generate accurate and compliant AI outputs.
4. Integrate responsible AI practices into research administration workflows.
5. Identify institutional strategies for fostering AI readiness and governance.

## Course Structure

- 5 modules, 2–3 hours total
- Comprised of reading, short videos, interactive games, mini module quizzes, and a final comprehensive assessment
- Completion certification
- Pre / Post surveys (part of NSF external evaluation plan)

## Modules

- Module 1: Introduction to AI in Research Administration
- Module 2: Ethical and Responsible AI Use
- Module 3: Prompt Engineering for Research Administration
- Module 4: Compliance, Risk, and Institutional Governance
- Module 5: AI Use Cases

## Module 1: Introduction to AI in Research Administration

**Purpose:** Build foundational understanding of AI and its relevance to research administration.

**Topics:**

- What is Artificial Intelligence? Overview and key terms
- How AI is transforming research administration processes?
- Common myths and misconceptions
- Is it an AI task or a Data Science task?
- Institutional examples of AI adoption in research administration
- Activity: Interactive quiz on AI Myths vs. Facts

## Module 2: Ethical and Responsible AI Use

**Purpose:** Explore ethical and responsible AI use in administrative and research contexts.

**Topics:**

- AI bias, transparency, and explainability
- Privacy and data security implications
  - Review FERPA, HIPAA, GDPR
- Authorship, originality, and intellectual property
  - Emphasize protection of proposal content
- Communicating/Disclosing AI Use
- Guidance from NSF, NIH, OSTP, etc. on responsible AI use
  - Show current NSF Guidance as example
- Activity: Case study — argue the ethical implications of a specific AI scenario

## Module 3: Prompt Development for Research Administration

**Purpose:** Learn how to communicate effectively with AI tools to produce accurate, relevant, and compliant outputs.

**Topics:**

- What is prompt engineering and why it matters
- Structure of an effective prompt (context, instruction, format, constraints)
  - TaMPER Framework
- Strategies for refining outputs ("iterative prompting")
  - Promptulus game: <https://n8-layman.shinyapps.io/promptulus/>
- Examples tailored to research administration
  - Drafting budget justifications or internal forms
  - Summarizing funding announcements
  - Creating training content or communication drafts
  - Reviewing proposal text for clarity or compliance tone
- Managing limitations and verifying accuracy of AI outputs
- Activity: Hands-on practice — participants refine a series of prompts for a sample administrative task (e.g., internal routing memo or budget narrative)

## Module 4: Compliance, Risk, and Institutional Governance

**Purpose:** Connect AI literacy with compliance and institutional oversight.

**Topics:**

- Regulatory touchpoints: data management, export controls, and research security
- Institutional policies and acceptable use
- Governance frameworks and risk management strategies
- Building accountability and transparency in AI-assisted processes
- Coordinating with campus services (IT, etc.) for AI adoption
- Activity: Discussion board — share how your institution could integrate governance for AI tools used in research administration
